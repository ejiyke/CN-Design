Philosophy

Design Principles

Four foundational tenets guide every component, pattern, and token decision in the CN Ecosystem.

🎯

Utility-First Clarity

Dense marketplace listings require instant legibility. High-contrast typography and clear visual signifiers make prices and locations scan fast.

📐

Modular 4px Hierarchy

From 4px micro-paddings to 80px page margins, every component adheres strictly to a predictable base-4 rhythmic grid.

🛡️

Marketplace Trust Signals

Built-in verified merchant badges, buyer-seller chat threads, and Nigerian Naira (₦) currency formatting anchor transaction confidence.

Icon Code Standards & Usage SVG Best Practices
1
Optical Sizing & Keyline Alignment: Always align icons to a 24×24px bounding box. In smaller containers (like inline buttons), scale proportionally to 16px or 20px using the provided size classes.
2
Token-Inherited currentColor: Never hardcode fill or stroke hex colors in the SVG. Rely on stroke="currentColor" so the icon inherits context-appropriate CSS color tokens across dark mode and state transitions.
3
Accessibility & Semantics: For decorative icons in buttons with accompanying text, use aria-hidden="true". For standalone icon buttons, supply an explicit aria-label or screen-reader accessible label.

✓ Do: Maintain Contrast

Place the logo over clean white, light gray (#F7F7F7), or deep slate backgrounds (#0F172A) to ensure maximum readability.

✓ Do: Preserve Proportions

Always scale the wordmark and favicon proportionally. Never adjust horizontal or vertical scale independently.

✕ Don't: Alter Brand Colors

Do not recolor the green letters, orange dot, or dark text. Brand recognition relies on exact chromatic consistency.

✕ Don't: Crowd Clearspace

Do not place buttons, text, or conflicting graphics within the 16px clearspace perimeter around the logo boundary.
